Novawall Installation

American Contractors Corp. is proud to be the exclusive partner of Novawall products for the New England region.

Novawall is the time-tested standard for exquisite fabric wall and ceiling applications in almost any environment. The patented site-fabricated system molds and adapts to your ideas, ensuring a meticulous finish — backed by the longest warranty in the industry, trained and certified installers, and an international network of distributors.

LAQFOIL Installation

LAQFOIL Stretch Ceiling is an innovative ceiling and wall surface that gives you massive freedom in design with minimal installation and upkeep hassle. The system is a PVC or Descor (canvas-like) membrane that stretches and clips to an aluminum perimeter track — so it can be installed without removing existing furniture, and used to hide existing popcorn ceilings and wires.

The PVC membrane is about as thin and lightweight as a building material can be — 0.12–0.14 mm thick — available in translucent and perforated acoustic versions, and in over 280 colors.

LAQFOIL designs accommodate existing light fixtures, ideal for low or uneven ceilings. Choose matte, satin, metallic, or gloss — the latter heightens and brightens the space considerably.
